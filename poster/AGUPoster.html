Coincident Anomalies in Ten Years of Photic Zone Profiles An Interpretive Framework for OOI Shallow Profiler Data

R. Fatland [+ co-authors TBD]

University of Washington · OOI Regional Cabled Array  |  AGU26 · Session OS025 · Abstract 2097211

Three OOI Regional Cabled Array shallow profilers have produced continuous 9-per-day profiles of the upper 200 m since 2015 — a decade-long synoptic characterization of the Northeast Pacific photic zone. We present visualization, analysis, and an interpretive framework for these 15-parameter time series (CTD, bio-optical, biogeochemical), with a reproducible data-cleaning workflow. The framework centers on three forms of anomaly coincidence: self (a single sensor, repeated across profiles), inter-sensor (across multiple sensors), and corroborated (confirmed by external sources — deep profilers, circulation models, remote sensing, Argo). Anomalies are defined against per-sensor mean±std baseline envelopes over seasonal/annual/multi-year windows (published with the tools). We show self- and inter-sensor coincident signals at/near the pycnocline interpreted as internal waves and coherent water-mass emplacement persisting over multiple tidal cycles. Published as an open-science GitHub repository with a step-by-step acquisition→analysis workflow. E.g. buoyancy frequency $N^2 = -\tfrac{g}{\rho_0}\,\dfrac{\partial \rho}{\partial z}$.
